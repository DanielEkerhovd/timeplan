Settings — the content, not the tabs

Each tab shows the thing you are setting up, the way it actually looks in the app. That fills the page, and it tells you what the setting does without a paragraph explaining it.

Week plan

The week itself is the interface

Seven day columns across the top, like the week your team sees. The switch under each day turns it off. The blocks live in bands under the days they belong to — Mon–Fri share one set, Sat–Sun another — and under it all, a preview of the buttons everyone will tap.
