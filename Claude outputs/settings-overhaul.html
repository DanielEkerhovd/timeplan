Settings — two ways to lay it out

Same content in both, merged and tightened: one Time slots card instead of two, compact rows, roles in two columns, and deleting the team pulled out into its own block.

Tabs at the top

One row of tabs, and the page under it is short enough to see at once. Same pattern as My week / Plan week, so it is already familiar. The tab you are on is not in the URL unless we put it there.
