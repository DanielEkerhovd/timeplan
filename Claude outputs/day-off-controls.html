Taking a day off — four controls

Same grid, four ways to reach the same action. Everything here is live: click around.

Now

A chip under every date

Seven buttons for something you use a few times a year. They sit in the header row, so they push the grid down and compete with the dates.

Option A

Only when you point at the day

A quiet round button that appears when the mouse is over the day, and stays visible on a day that is already off, so you can always put it back. Nothing to see the rest of the time.

Hover a date to see it. Wednesday is off, so its button stays.

Option B

One row above the grid

All seven days in a single control, out of the grid. It reads as one sentence — these are the days we are not playing — and it is the same control on phone and desktop.

Option C

Click the date itself

The date becomes the button and opens a small menu. Nothing is added to the layout at all, but you have to know it is there — the header does not look clickable until you touch it.
